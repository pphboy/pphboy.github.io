# Product demo

**Create. Evolve. Take it anywhere.**

This 70-second video follows one idea as AI turns it into a working content studio, adds a publishing calendar while keeping the original ideas, and moves the entire Work into another Piwork environment.

<ProductDemo />

## What the video shows

1. **Create:** AI builds **My Creative Studio** inside Piwork, with idea cards, categories, status, search, and persistent data.
2. **Evolve:** AI adds a publishing calendar to the same application, preserving the six original ideas. A calendar entry opens an existing record.
3. **Move:** the Work is stopped and exported as a `.work` file, then imported into an independent Piwork installation and explicitly started. The application, calendar, and original records are verified again.

## Recording details

The footage comes from real Piwork 0.0.7 operations. Model execution and deployment waits are shortened in the edit.

Migration was verified between two independent Piwork installations on the same Linux x86-64 host, with compatible runtime images and model credentials configured in each environment.
