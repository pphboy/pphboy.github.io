Product demo ​

Create. Evolve. Take it anywhere.

Watch Piwork: One AI Workspace, Five Real Demos (2:25). Start with a Work, describe what you want to do, check the results, and keep improving.

Watch on YouTube · 2:25

Five ways to use a Work ​

The video introduces:

  1. ProMAIC: learn with courses, track progress, and turn a study plan into a calendar.
  2. Dot Desktop: edit files, browse the web, and check saved results.
  3. PiBeat: describe a musical idea, generate a track, and hear the result.
  4. PiCut: turn footage and editing instructions into a finished video.
  5. PiAbyss: move, explore, and fight in a playable game.

The workflow also explains how to export a workspace as a .work file, import it into another Piwork installation, and continue. Stop the Work before exporting; after importing, explicitly start it. See your first Work for the steps and data boundaries.
