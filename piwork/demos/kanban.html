ABOUT THIS DEMO

A starting point for your own work.

  • Drag task cards between To do, In progress and Done.
  • Focus your work with projects, labels and search.
  • Adjust dates in Timeline and keep discussion in task notes.

What is included

The app, example data and an English Agent demo conversation. After importing, click Start and wait for Services to become ready. Choose Session 3 for a short introduction.
